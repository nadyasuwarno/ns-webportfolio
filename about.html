Different rooms, same job: make the unfamiliar feel like somewhere you belong.

Hey, I'm Nadya. I was born and raised in Jakarta, and I've spent the years since finding my footing in rooms that felt bigger than me, then helping other people find theirs.

Before Parsons, I'd already worn a lot of hats: growing an online community for a dance agency in Seoul, marketing films in Jakarta, running events for Indonesian students in New York.

Now I do it through product design. I believe anything can be learned if it's designed well. Curiosity gets people in the door. Demystification shows them how it works. Enablement lets them walk on their own.
