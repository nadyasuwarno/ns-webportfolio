01Overview

Every spring, Parsons Design & Technology seniors show the work of their final year: films, games, installations, live performances, and almost nothing in common on the surface. The show needs one name and one identity. It has to feel like the whole class without flattening anyone's work into sameness.

02Concept development

[ Process here — how you gathered the class's themes. A survey, reading thesis statements, a workshop? What names did you try first? ]

As we looked across the work, a pattern kept surfacing. Classmates were taking things apart and putting them back together: memories, cities, bodies, systems, stories. The show's statement puts it best: each work explores melting and mending, reconstructing and reimagining. Together, a collective is born.

A mosaic is exactly that: pieces that were broken become something whole. Each tile keeps its own colour and shape, and only together do you see the picture. That became our red string, the one idea every student's work could hang on.
